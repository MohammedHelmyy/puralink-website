Compliance

How patient data is handled, in plain terms

Every relationship with a facility starts with a signed data agreement, and every workflow is scoped to only the access that agreement covers.

Our working commitments

These apply to every facility relationship, without exception.

1

Signed agreement before any access

Data access begins only after a facility has authorized it and the corresponding agreement is signed. No data is accessed on assumed or informal permission.

2

Scoped strictly to the agreed use

We access only the specific patient data needed to track the chronic conditions in scope — nothing broader than what the facility has agreed to.

3

Encrypted in transit and at rest

Patient data is encrypted both while it moves between systems and while it's stored, at every stage of the monthly cycle.

4

Access limited to those who need it

Only the specific staff involved in producing a patient's report — and the reviewing physician — can see that patient's data.

5

Physician review on every report

No report is finalized or delivered without a licensed physician reviewing it first.

6

Retained only as long as needed

Data is kept only for as long as the facility relationship and applicable recordkeeping requirements call for, and no longer.

Working within U.S. health data law

For facilities and data covered under U.S. health privacy law, our agreements and access methods are structured to operate within that framework — including formal authorization from the facility for any system we connect to, and a signed agreement covering our role in handling that data.

We don't access a facility's clinical systems through any method the system provider hasn't approved. Where a system requires a partner or integration approval process, we go through it.

Questions from your compliance team

If your facility's compliance or IT team wants to review our data handling process before signing anything, we're glad to walk through it directly.
