Compliance

Privacy and security built into every CCM workflow

PuraLink treats protected health information as a clinical responsibility. Our controls are designed to protect patient data throughout collection, care coordination, reporting, and retention.

Our HIPAA compliance commitment

Security is part of the service, not an add-on after implementation.

1

Business Associate Agreements

Before handling PHI, PuraLink executes the appropriate Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with the covered entity. Roles, permitted uses, safeguards, and incident responsibilities are documented up front.

2

Minimum-necessary access

Access is limited to the patient information needed for the agreed CCM services. Permissions are reviewed and adjusted as roles and workflows change.

3

End-to-end data encryption

PHI is encrypted end to end in transit and at rest across the PuraLink workflow, including collection, processing, storage, and report delivery.

4

Access controls and MFA

Role-based access controls and multi-factor authentication help ensure that only authorized care managers and approved personnel can view patient metrics.

5

Complete audit logging

Access and relevant system activity are logged so authorized teams can review who accessed information, when, and for what operational purpose.

6

Risk assessments and oversight

Regular risk assessments, workforce safeguards, and documented procedures help us identify weaknesses early and maintain a zero-violation compliance standard.

A security program your compliance team can examine

PuraLink combines end-to-end encryption, role-based access, MFA, audit logs, regular risk assessments, and signed BAAs to protect PHI throughout the CCM reporting cycle.

We authorize access before work begins, use only approved system connections, and document the controls that govern each provider relationship. Our goal is straightforward: zero compliance violations and no ambiguity about who can access patient information.

Questions from your compliance or IT team?

We are prepared to review our data handling, access controls, BAA process, and security responsibilities with your team before implementation.
