CCM support that keeps the care plan moving

PuraLink extends your team between appointments with disciplined tracking, meaningful monthly touchpoints, and clear clinical reporting.

Continuous patient tracking

We monitor enrolled patients throughout the month, organize daily measurements, and surface meaningful changes across conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, COPD, heart failure, and chronic kidney disease.

Comprehensive medical reporting

Each patient receives a structured monthly status report with measurements, engagement, care activity, medication context, and documented follow-up. Reports are prepared for physician review and reimbursement workflows.

Proactive patient engagement

Regular touchpoints help patients stay connected to their care plan. When a trend needs attention, your team receives a specific, dated note instead of another disconnected data point.

The CCM care cycle

A reliable operating rhythm that helps your team deliver meaningful care every month and document it clearly.

1

Enroll and establish baseline

We confirm eligibility, care priorities, goals, and the baseline information needed for ongoing monitoring.

2

Track daily measurements

Patient readings, symptoms, medication updates, and care activity are collected and organized throughout the month.

3

Coordinate monthly touchpoints

Patients receive consistent outreach and support so questions are addressed and participation does not quietly lapse.

4

Review clinical trends

Our care team organizes changes and follow-up needs so your practice can make informed decisions efficiently.

5

Receive the monthly report

You receive a complete, audit-ready patient status report to review, document, and submit through your reimbursement process.

Reports designed for clinical action

PuraLink turns a month of patient activity into a concise clinical record your team can review quickly and use confidently.

  • Complete and organized. Includes the patient status, measurements, care activity, engagement, and follow-up context for the reporting period.
  • Specific about change. Highlights what moved, what may require attention, and how the current month compares with prior data.
  • Built for reimbursement workflows. Delivered in a consistent, audit-ready format that supports physician review and submission.
