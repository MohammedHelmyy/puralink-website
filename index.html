Built for one setting: patients already under your roof

Not a general telehealth platform — a follow-up service scoped to hospitals and residential facilities managing chronic disease over long stays.

Chronic condition tracking

We follow each patient's diagnosed chronic conditions — diabetes, hypertension, COPD, renal disease, and similar — against their facility record, month after month, for the duration of their stay.

Monthly medical reports

Every case receives one structured report per month: current status, medication review, and a plain comparison against the prior month, reviewed and signed by a physician before it reaches you.

Problem notes, not just data

When something needs attention — a reading trending the wrong way, a medication concern — it's written up as a specific, dated problem note, not buried in a spreadsheet.

The monthly cycle

The same five steps, run for every patient, every month, for as long as they're in your care.

1

Admission review

We record the chronic conditions on file and establish the baseline we'll track against.

2

Monthly check-in

Current vitals, medications, and condition-specific markers are reviewed against the baseline and prior months.

3

Problem notes drafted

Any concerning change is written up as a specific, dated note describing what changed and why it matters.

4

Physician review

A licensed physician reviews the draft report and notes, and signs off before anything is finalized.

5

Report delivered

The finished monthly report reaches your facility's care team, ready for the patient's file.

A problem note looks like this

Short, dated, specific — written so a nurse or physician can act on it in the time it takes to read it, not interpret it.

  • Dated and case-specific. Tied to a single month and a single chronic condition, never a general summary.
  • States the change, not just the number. What moved, from what, to what, and over what period.
  • Physician-reviewed before delivery. Never sent to a facility without clinical sign-off.
