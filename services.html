Services

Everything covered in the monthly cycle

Five services, run continuously for every chronic-condition patient on your census, from admission through discharge.

01 · Ongoing

Chronic condition monitoring

We track the specific chronic conditions already on a patient's chart — diabetes, hypertension, COPD, chronic kidney disease, heart failure, and similar long-term conditions — against a baseline set at admission.

  • Condition-specific markers. Each condition is followed using the indicators that actually matter for it, not a generic vitals sheet.
  • Baseline set at intake. Every comparison is made against where the patient started, not an arbitrary norm.
  • Runs for the length of stay. Tracking continues month to month for as long as the patient remains in your facility.

02 · Monthly

Structured medical reports

One report per patient, per month, following the same structure every time — so your care team knows exactly where to look, regardless of which patient or which month.

  • Consistent format. The same five sections every month, for every patient.
  • Delivered as a signed PDF. Ready to file directly into the patient's record.
03 · As needed

Problem note flagging

When a condition moves in the wrong direction, it's written up as a specific, dated problem note — not left for someone to notice buried in a long report.

  • Written for action. States what changed, from what baseline, and what it likely means.
  • Never silent. If nothing needs attention, the report says so explicitly — an empty section is not left to guesswork.

Every report, every month

No report reaches your facility without a licensed physician reviewing the draft, checking the clinical reasoning, and signing off on the final version.

04 · Built in

Physician review, on every report

Our internal process routes every draft report through a physician before it's finalized. Nothing is sent out as an automated conclusion.

05 · At onboarding

Facility onboarding and fit

Before the first monthly cycle starts, we work with your care team to agree on which conditions to track, how reports should be routed, and who reviews them on your side.

  • Set up around your process. We fit into how your care team already works, not the other way around.
  • Clear from day one. Scope, cadence, and delivery are agreed before tracking begins.
