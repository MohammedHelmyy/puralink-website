Services

CCM support built around the work physicians cannot afford to lose

PuraLink helps your practice maintain consistent patient monitoring, complete monthly care coordination, and produce documentation your clinical and revenue-cycle teams can use.

01 · Ongoing

Continuous chronic-care monitoring

PuraLink helps your team keep a current view of patients living with diabetes, hypertension, COPD, heart failure, kidney disease, and other chronic conditions.

  • Daily measurements organized. Relevant readings and patient updates are brought together for review.
  • Monthly status maintained. Each patient has a clear record of progress, open concerns, and care activity.
  • Care gaps made visible. Missed touchpoints and concerning trends are easier for your team to address.

02 · Monthly

High-quality monthly reports

Receive a consistent, comprehensive view of each patient's monthly CCM activity and status, designed to support clinical review, audit readiness, and reimbursement workflows.

  • Clear clinical structure. Patient status, measurements, interventions, medication context, and unresolved issues are easy to locate.
  • Ready for physician review. Reports are delivered in a format your team can incorporate into its documentation process.
03 · As needed

Care-gap and escalation notes

When a patient needs attention, the relevant change is documented clearly and routed back to the appropriate clinical team instead of being buried in raw data.

  • Specific and dated. Records what changed, when it changed, and which condition or care task is affected.
  • Supports proactive care. Gives your team a clear starting point for follow-up and patient outreach.

04 · Built in

Physician-ready, reimbursement-minded documentation

PuraLink gives the physician a concise record of the month's work, patient status, and outstanding needs so clinical judgment remains central to care and reimbursement submission.

05 · At onboarding

Implementation that fits your practice

We align the workflow with your eligible patient population, documentation standards, care-team responsibilities, and preferred reporting process before the first month begins.

  • Set up around your process. We fit into how your care team already works, not the other way around.
  • Clear from day one. Scope, cadence, and delivery are agreed before tracking begins.
