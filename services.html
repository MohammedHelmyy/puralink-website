Services

A dependable operating layer for Chronic Care Management

PuraLink handles the recurring work behind effective CCM: patient tracking, daily measurements, monthly monitoring, care coordination, and clear reporting back to your practice.

01 · Ongoing

Continuous patient tracking

We keep a current view of each enrolled patient’s chronic conditions, daily measurements, symptoms, medications, and care activity so your team can see the full month, not just the next appointment.

  • Condition-specific markers. We organize the measures and symptoms relevant to each patient’s care plan.
  • Daily visibility. Measurements and updates are captured throughout the month, helping surface change earlier.
  • Fewer gaps in follow-up. Consistent outreach keeps patients engaged and gives your team a clearer record of care.

02 · Monthly

Comprehensive medical reporting

At the end of each cycle, your practice receives a complete patient status report that turns ongoing care activity into documentation your clinicians can review and your billing team can use.

  • Audit-ready detail. Clear dates, measurements, interventions, touchpoints, and patient status in one organized record.
  • Reimbursement workflow support. Documentation is structured to help your team submit eligible CCM work through its established payer process.
03 · As needed

Proactive follow-up coordination

When a measurement changes or a patient needs additional attention, PuraLink records the signal clearly and routes the next-step context to the people responsible for care.

  • Written for action. States what changed, over what period, and why it may matter to the care plan.
  • Patient-centered. Regular outreach helps address questions, reinforce the plan, and reduce patient drop-off.

Clear reporting for physician review

Your clinicians receive the information they need to evaluate the patient’s status, determine next steps, and complete their own documentation and reimbursement process.

04 · Built in

Reporting that respects clinical judgment

PuraLink organizes the record and highlights relevant changes. The treating provider remains responsible for clinical decisions, patient care, and final submission.

05 · At onboarding

Practice onboarding and fit

Before enrollment begins, we align with your team on patient eligibility, care priorities, measurement workflows, escalation paths, report delivery, and reimbursement documentation needs.

  • Set up around your process. We fit into how your physicians, nurses, and billing team already work.
  • Clear from day one. Scope, cadence, access, escalation, and report delivery are agreed before tracking begins.
