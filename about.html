About us

A follow-up service built around one job

PuraLink exists to make sure chronic conditions don't get lost in the day-to-day of a long hospital or facility stay. That's the whole scope — not a general health platform.

Why we started here

Patients admitted with chronic conditions are usually stabilized quickly for the reason they came in — but the chronic condition itself often gets a lighter touch once the acute issue is handled. Over a stay of several months, that's a long gap without a dedicated, structured check-in on the underlying condition.

PuraLink fills exactly that gap: a monthly, physician-reviewed check on the chronic conditions a patient came in with, for as long as they're in your care.

What we don't do
Replace the treating physician or care teamWe report to them, not around them
Deliver unreviewed, automated conclusionsEvery report is physician-signed
Handle acute, in-the-moment careThat stays with your facility's own team

What guides the work

Four principles that shape every report we produce.

Specific over general

A report that names the exact change in the exact condition is worth more than a broad wellness summary.

Reviewed, not automated

A physician reads and signs every report before it reaches a facility. No exceptions.

Consistent format

The same report structure every month means your team spends less time interpreting and more time acting.

Scoped access

We work with exactly the data needed for the conditions being tracked, through the access your facility authorizes.

Working with your facility

We onboard alongside your care team, agree on scope, and start the monthly cycle from an agreed admission baseline.

How we work with a facility

  • You stay the treating provider. PuraLink is a follow-up and reporting layer, not a replacement for clinical care.
  • Scope is agreed up front. Which conditions, which patients, and how reports are routed — settled before tracking starts.
  • One point of contact. A single monthly report per patient, delivered to the person your facility designates.
