About us

A practical extension of your chronic-care team

PuraLink helps physicians and healthcare organizations deliver consistent CCM follow-up without adding another layer of administrative work to an already stretched team.

Why PuraLink exists

Chronic care depends on consistency: regular patient contact, current measurements, medication awareness, documented interventions, and timely escalation when something changes. In practice, those activities can compete with visits, staffing demands, and administrative deadlines.

PuraLink helps close that operational gap. We organize the ongoing work, return a clear monthly view to the physician, and help your team keep eligible patients engaged in care.

What we don't do
Replace the treating physicianWe support clinical judgment, not replace it
Make independent diagnosesClinical decisions remain with your providers
Add another disconnected systemWe align the workflow with your team

What guides the work

Four principles that shape every patient touchpoint, report, and provider relationship.

Clinical clarity

Reports identify the patient status, care activity, changes, and open needs your team must understand.

Human review matters

Structured data supports the workflow, but provider judgment remains central to interpretation and action.

Reliable cadence

Consistent monthly touchpoints help prevent eligible patients from disappearing between appointments.

Privacy by design

Access is limited to authorized users and the information required to perform the agreed care-coordination work.

Working with your facility

We onboard alongside your care team, agree on scope, and start the monthly cycle from an agreed admission baseline.
