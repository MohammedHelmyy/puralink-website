About us

The operational partner behind more consistent chronic care

PuraLink helps physicians and healthcare organizations deliver Chronic Care Management with the consistency patients need and the documentation providers require.

Why PuraLink exists

Chronic care does not happen only during office visits. Patients need regular monitoring, clear communication, and practical support between appointments. Meanwhile, physicians and clinic teams are expected to coordinate that work while managing a full clinical and administrative load.

PuraLink provides the operating support to keep monthly CCM activity moving: tracking patient status, coordinating touchpoints, organizing daily measurements, and returning a complete report to the provider.

What we don't do
Replace the treating physician or care teamWe support their decisions, not replace them
Make unsupported clinical decisionsWe organize information for provider review
Wait for patients to fall out of careWe maintain regular monthly touchpoints

What guides the work

Four principles that shape every patient interaction and report.

Patients over paperwork

Documentation matters because it reflects care delivered. We keep the patient’s needs at the center of every monthly cycle.

Useful over noisy

We surface the measurements, trends, and follow-up details that help providers decide what deserves attention.

Consistent by design

A dependable monthly structure makes it easier for clinical and billing teams to review, document, and submit eligible work.

Secure by default

Patient information is handled through controlled access, encryption, MFA, audit logs, risk reviews, and signed BAAs.

Working with your facility

We onboard alongside your care team, agree on scope, and start the monthly cycle from an agreed admission baseline.

How we work with your practice

  • You stay the treating provider. PuraLink supports care coordination and reporting; your clinicians retain clinical judgment and responsibility.
  • Scope is agreed up front. Eligibility, care priorities, access, escalation, and report delivery are settled before tracking starts.
  • Your workflow stays visible. Your team receives organized monthly information that supports care decisions and reimbursement documentation.
