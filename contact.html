Common questions

Do you require a signed agreement before any data access?

Yes. We do not access provider systems or patient data until authorization, approved access methods, and the appropriate signed BAA are in place.

Who reviews the monthly reports before we receive them?

PuraLink organizes the monthly record for provider review. The treating physician retains clinical judgment and responsibility for final documentation and submission.

Can we choose which chronic conditions are tracked?

Yes. Eligibility, conditions, measurements, outreach cadence, escalation paths, and report delivery are agreed with your team before enrollment begins.

How is patient data protected?

PHI is encrypted in transit and at rest. Role-based access, MFA, audit logs, regular risk assessments, and signed BAAs help ensure only authorized personnel can access patient metrics. See our HIPAA compliance page for details.
