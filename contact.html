Questions providers often ask

How does PuraLink support CCM reimbursement?

PuraLink helps organize eligible patient monitoring, monthly care-coordination activity, and supporting documentation. Your clinicians and billing team remain responsible for confirming eligibility, clinical requirements, and submission decisions.

What does the monthly report include?

Reports can include patient status, relevant measurements, care activity, medication context, identified gaps, unresolved concerns, and the information your team agrees to include during implementation.

Can PuraLink fit our existing workflow?

Yes. During onboarding, we define responsibilities, patient scope, communication cadence, reporting format, and routing so the workflow supports your existing clinical operations.

How is patient data protected?

Our approach includes encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, MFA, audit logs, risk assessments, and BAAs where applicable. See our security and compliance page for details.
